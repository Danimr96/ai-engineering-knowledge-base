# Entropy

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "Randomness"

**What it actually means:** The average surprise across all outcomes of a distribution. Measures irreducible uncertainty.
