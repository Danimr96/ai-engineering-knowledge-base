# One-hot encoding

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "Binary columns for categories"

**What it actually means:** Representing class k as a vector of zeros with a 1 at position k
