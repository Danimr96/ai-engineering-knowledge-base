**Community:** [[Communities/94 Positional encoding · RoPE · YaRN|94 Positional encoding · RoPE · YaRN]]

> *What people say:* "Fourier features"

**What it actually means:** Encode each coordinate into sin/cos at multiple frequencies to overcome MLP low-frequency bias
