# Curse of dimensionality

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "Too many features"

**What it actually means:** Distances, volumes, and data density all behave counterintuitively as dimensions grow. Models need exponentially more data to compensate.
