# ORPO

**Community:** [[Communities/45 Implicit reward · KTO · ORPO|45 Implicit reward · KTO · ORPO]]

> *What people say:* "One-step alignment"

**What it actually means:** Odds Ratio Preference Optimization: combines SFT and alignment into a single training loop by adding a preference term to the SFT loss
