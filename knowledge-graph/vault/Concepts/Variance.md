# Variance

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "How spread out"

**What it actually means:** The expected squared deviation from the mean. High variance = noisy, unstable estimates
