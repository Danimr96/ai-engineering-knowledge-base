# Sigmoid

**Community:** [[Communities/27 Decision boundary · Bias · ELBO|27 Decision boundary · Bias · ELBO]]

> *What people say:* "The S-curve"

**What it actually means:** sigma(z) = 1/(1+e^(-z)), squashes any real number to (0,1), smooth and differentiable everywhere
