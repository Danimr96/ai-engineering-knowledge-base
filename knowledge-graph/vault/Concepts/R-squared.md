# R-squared

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "How good the fit is"

**What it actually means:** The fraction of variance in y explained by the model, ranging from negative infinity to 1.0
