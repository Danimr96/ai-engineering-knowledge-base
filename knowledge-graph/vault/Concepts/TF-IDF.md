# TF-IDF

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "Fancy word counts"

**What it actually means:** Term Frequency times Inverse Document Frequency: words weighted by how distinctive they are across the corpus
