# Fallback Chain

**Community:** [[Communities/19 CIMD · DCR · Resource indicator|19 CIMD · DCR · Resource indicator]]

> *What people say:* "Model cascade"

**What it actually means:** An ordered list of models tried in sequence -- when the primary fails, fall through to cheaper or more available alternatives
