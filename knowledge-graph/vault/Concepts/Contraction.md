# Contraction

**Community:** [[Communities/34 Tensor · CUDA · Mixed Precision|34 Tensor · CUDA · Mixed Precision]]

> *What people say:* "Summing over an index"

**What it actually means:** The general operation where a shared index between tensors is multiplied and summed, producing a lower-rank result
