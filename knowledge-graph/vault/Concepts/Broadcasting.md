# Broadcasting

**Community:** [[Communities/34 Tensor · CUDA · Mixed Precision|34 Tensor · CUDA · Mixed Precision]]

> *What people say:* "Magic shape fixing"

**What it actually means:** Stretching a smaller array to match a larger one by repeating along missing dimensions.
