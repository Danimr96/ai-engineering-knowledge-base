# Determinant

**Community:** [[Communities/08 Backpropagation · Gradient · Activation Function|08 Backpropagation · Gradient · Activation Function]]

> *What people say:* "Some number from the matrix"

**What it actually means:** Measures how much the matrix scales area (2D) or volume (3D). Zero means the transformation crushes a dimension.
