# Bias

**Community:** [[Communities/27 Decision boundary · Bias · ELBO|27 Decision boundary · Bias · ELBO]]

> *What people say:* "The model is too simple"

**What it actually means:** Systematic error from wrong assumptions. The gap between the average model prediction and truth.
