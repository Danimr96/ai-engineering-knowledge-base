# Monte Carlo

**Community:** [[Communities/49 MCMC · Metropolis-Hastings · Importance sampling|49 MCMC · Metropolis-Hastings · Importance sampling]]

> *What people say:* "Average random samples"

**What it actually means:** Approximate integrals as sample averages. Error O(1/sqrt(N)) regardless of dimension
