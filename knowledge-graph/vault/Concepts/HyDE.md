# HyDE

**Community:** [[Communities/26 BM25 · Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) · Bi-encoder|26 BM25 · Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) · Bi-encoder]]

> *What people say:* "Search with a fake answer"

**What it actually means:** Generate a hypothetical answer to the query, embed it, and search for real documents similar to it
