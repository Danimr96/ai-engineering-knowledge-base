# Laplace smoothing

**Community:** [[Communities/62 Laplace smoothing · Likelihood · Log probability|62 Laplace smoothing · Likelihood · Log probability]]

> *What people say:* "Add-one smoothing"

**What it actually means:** Adding a small count to every feature to prevent zero probabilities from unseen data.
