# Anomaly

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "An outlier"

**What it actually means:** A data point that does not conform to the expected pattern, identified as noise by DBSCAN or low-probability by GMM
