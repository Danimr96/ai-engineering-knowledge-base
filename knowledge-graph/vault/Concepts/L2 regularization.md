# L2 regularization

**Community:** [[Communities/75 L2 regularization · PRM · Reward Model|75 L2 regularization · PRM · Reward Model]]

> *What people say:* "Ridge" or "weight decay"

**What it actually means:** Adding the squared L2 norm of weights to the loss. Shrinks weights toward zero without sparsity
