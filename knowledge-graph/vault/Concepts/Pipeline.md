# Pipeline

**Community:** [[Communities/86 DVC · Pipeline|86 DVC · Pipeline]]

> *What people say:* "Chain of transforms + model"

**What it actually means:** An ordered sequence of fitted transformers and a model, applied as one unit to prevent leakage
