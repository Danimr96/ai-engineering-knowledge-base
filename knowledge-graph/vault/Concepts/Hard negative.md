# Hard negative

**Community:** [[Communities/40 NaFlex · Backbone · Hard negative|40 NaFlex · Backbone · Hard negative]]

> *What people say:* "Tough distractor"

**What it actually means:** A negative similar enough to the positive that the model has to work to separate them
