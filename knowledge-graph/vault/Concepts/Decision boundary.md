# Decision boundary

**Community:** [[Communities/27 Decision boundary · Bias · ELBO|27 Decision boundary · Bias · ELBO]]

> *What people say:* "The dividing line"

**What it actually means:** The surface where the model's output probability equals 0.5, separating predicted classes
