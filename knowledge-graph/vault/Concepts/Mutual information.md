# Mutual information

**Community:** [[Communities/04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage|04 Overfitting · Underfitting · Data Leakage]]

> *What people say:* "How related are X and Y"

**What it actually means:** Reduction in uncertainty about X from knowing Y. Zero means independent.
