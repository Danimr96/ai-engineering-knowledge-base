# Log probability

**Community:** [[Communities/62 Laplace smoothing · Likelihood · Log probability|62 Laplace smoothing · Likelihood · Log probability]]

> *What people say:* "Log of the probability"

**What it actually means:** log P(x). Turns products into sums, preventing numerical underflow in long sequences
