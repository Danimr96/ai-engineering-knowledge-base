**Community:** [[Communities/67 Central Limit Theorem · Confidence interval · Covariance matrix|67 Central Limit Theorem · Confidence interval · Covariance matrix]]

> *What people say:* "Averages become normal"

**What it actually means:** The mean of many independent samples converges to a normal distribution regardless of the source
