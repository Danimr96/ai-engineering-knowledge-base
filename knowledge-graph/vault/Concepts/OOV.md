# OOV

**Community:** [[Communities/56 NLLB · Constrained decoding · OOV|56 NLLB · Constrained decoding · OOV]]

> *What people say:* Out of vocabulary

**What it actually means:** Word not seen in training. Word2Vec cannot produce a vector for these.
