## Concepts taught

- [[Concepts/Vector|Vector]] `lesson term` — A list of numbers representing a point or direction in n-dimensional space
- [[Concepts/Matrix|Matrix]] `lesson term` — A transformation that maps vectors from one space to another
- [[Concepts/Dot product|Dot product]] `lesson term` — A measure of how aligned two vectors are -- the core of similarity search
- [[Concepts/Embedding|Embedding]] `glossary` — A vector that represents the meaning of something (word, image, user)
- Linear independence `lesson term` — No vector in the set can be written as a combination of the others
- [[Concepts/Rank|Rank]] `lesson term` — The number of linearly independent columns (or rows) in a matrix
- [[Concepts/Projection|Projection]] `lesson term` — The component of one vector in the direction of another
- Basis `lesson term` — A minimal set of independent vectors that span the space
- Orthonormal `lesson term` — Vectors that are mutually perpendicular and each have length 1
