# 18/05 Constitutional AI and RLAIF

> Bai et al. (arXiv:2212.08073, 2022) asked: what if we replaced the human labeler with an AI that reads a list of principles? Constitutional AI has two phases — self-critique and revision under a constitution, then RL from AI Feedback. The technique coined the term RLAIF and shipped in the Claude 1 post-training pipeline. On 21 January 2026 Anthropic published a rewritten Claude constitution: explanatory reasoning over prescriptive rules, a four-tier priority hierarchy, and the first major-lab formal acknowledgment of uncertainty about model moral status. Released under CC0 1.0.

## Concepts taught

- [[Concepts/Constitutional AI|Constitutional AI]] `lesson term` — Two-phase pipeline: self-critique-and-revise SFT, then RL from AI feedback
- [[Concepts/RLAIF|RLAIF]] `lesson term` — RL with preferences generated by an AI labeler; the rest of the pipeline is unchanged
- [[Concepts/Constitution|Constitution]] `lesson term` — An ordered list of natural-language rules the critique/labeler model consults
- Critique-and-revise `lesson term` — Produce response → critique under a principle → revise → SFT target
- Constitutional Classifier `lesson term` — Lightweight classifier that evaluates outputs against the constitution and blocks/logs
- Four-tier priority `lesson term` — 2026 Claude constitution hierarchy: catastrophic > platform > ethics > helpful
- Feedback model `lesson term` — The model that reads a principle and ranks a pair of completions
